Hi, I'm Armin. This is the first time I've created an API.

An API is how one piece of software asks another to do something. You send a request, it does a job, and it sends a response back. This page is my first one, and you can try it right here.

Mine does one small job: it takes your name and email, adds you to my list, and emails you a thank-you. Everything you type shows up in the request panel, so you can see exactly what gets sent before you send it.

  1. 1

    You fill in the form

    Your first name, last name and email become the request.

  2. 2

    You click Join me

    The request goes to my API, which lets me know and asks Resend to send your email.

  3. 3

    You get a response

    A thank-you shows up here and in your inbox.

It's a small start, and I'd like you to be part of it.
